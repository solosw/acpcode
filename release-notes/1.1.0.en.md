New
- Import upstream providers from a site's link; featured upstreams come as cards you can open.
- The model picker shows model and upstream logos; each model keeps its own effort and context, so switching sends no test request.
- Files mentioned in a chat open in your editor.
- Queued messages dock over the composer, to edit, send now or remove.
- Open Project under New Chat; the explorer keeps navigation history and the sidebar manages remote hosts.
- Settings' page list resizes and hides like the sidebar.
- MCP servers show whether they answer, and why one doesn't.
- Drag files out of the window on macOS.

Improved
- Command and pickers: a hover redraws two rows, a search line to click into, edges faded instead of ruled.
- Auto save follows files.autoSave, as VS Code does.
- Sharper Windows text (pixel-snapped sizes and an engine patch); OpenSSH 8.4 and newer asks for passwords.
- The chat's scrollbar scales and the reader stays where they are; hovers stay inside the window, clear of its edges.

Fixed
- Calls and commands a stopped turn leaves unanswered now fail instead of hanging.
- A remote project's changed files expand and open without a blank page or file-not-found (Windows).
- Multi-folder workspaces keep sending messages even when change review hangs.
- ACP chat startup no longer breaks the session being mounted, so switching conversations doesn't hang.
