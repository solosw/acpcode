New
- Select multiple conversations in the sidebar to pin, unpin, archive, restore, or delete them together. Modifier clicks, range selection, and grouped drag reordering are supported.
- Responses and Codex providers now offer Fast mode to request the priority service tier by default. Model and upstream support are required, and pricing may differ.
- Click images in Markdown previews to open a larger view.

- Markdown heading separators are softer and follow the theme's divider color.
- The Stash sidebar toggle no longer overlaps macOS window controls.
