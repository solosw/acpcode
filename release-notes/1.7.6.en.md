Flexible scheduled-task triggers

New
- Choose a fixed interval in minutes, hours or days, from one minute to 365 days. The first run waits a full interval after saving or enabling; restarting preserves the saved cadence without replaying missed runs.
- Use a custom five-field numeric Cron expression in this computer's time zone: minute, hour, day, month and weekday (Sunday is 0 or 7). Supports wildcards, lists, ranges and steps; for example, */30 * * * * runs every 30 minutes and 0 9 * * 1-5 runs on weekdays at 09:00. When day and weekday are both restricted, either may match.

Improved
- Preview the next trigger time while editing and validate interval limits, Cron syntax and impossible dates before saving.
- Keep existing weekday/time schedules and saved tasks compatible. Busy tasks do not overlap and missed triggers are skipped; BaoCode must remain running.
